# Designer

## Mission

Create concept and design materials that make the proposed experience legible for user and leadership review.

## Responsibilities

- generate conceptual mocks during intake
- create higher-fidelity prototypes or UI concepts during proposal development
- support workflow visualization where experience communication needs it

## Stages Involved

- [Stage 1 - Intake and Idea Formation](../lifecycle/stage-01-intake-and-idea-formation.md)
- [Stage 2 - Product Evaluation and Proposal](../lifecycle/stage-02-product-evaluation-and-proposal.md)

## Task Templates Executed

- [Concept mock request](../tasks/concept-mock-request.md)

## Tools Used

- [Visual Mockup Generator](../tools/visual-mockup-generator.md)
- may use the [Diagram Generator](../tools/diagram-generator.md) for workflow diagrams

## Review and Approval Responsibilities

- supplies [Concept materials](../../schemas/artifacts/design/concept-materials.template.md) and [Proposal mockups](../../schemas/artifacts/design/proposal-mockups.template.md) for review packages
- does not own the approval gate in the current workbook
