# Reflection and Dream

Remram uses two different ideas for knowledge improvement over time.

## Reflection

Reflection happens after a run completes.

Its job is to look back at what just happened and ask:

- what changed
- what should be remembered
- what should remain provisional

Reflection is the near-term write path. It captures signal while the interaction is still fresh.

## Dream

Dream is the slower reconciliation path.

Its job is to revisit accumulated knowledge and ask:

- what conflicts
- what repeats
- what has become stable enough to promote
- what should be demoted or discarded

Dream is about preventing drift and consolidating durable patterns.

## Why Both Matter

Without reflection, the system forgets.

Without dream, the system accumulates noise.

The combination creates a useful separation:

- reflection updates
- dream reconciles
