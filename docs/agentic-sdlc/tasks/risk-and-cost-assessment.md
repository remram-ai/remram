# Risk and Cost Assessment

## Purpose

Evaluate the proposal's impact, risk, and cost.

## Inputs

- candidate implementation approach

## Outputs

- risk summary
- cost considerations

## Steps

1. Evaluate system risk.
2. Evaluate cross-service impact.
3. Evaluate infrastructure changes.
4. Evaluate whether new services are required.
5. Evaluate token consumption or compute cost.

## Artifacts Created

- risk and cost sections in the proposal
