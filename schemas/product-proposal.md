# Product Proposal

## Category

Product artifact

## Canonical Location

- `/roadmap/proposals/<proposal>.md`

## Purpose

Define what should be built, how it might be built, and what risks or costs are involved.

## Required Contents

- executive summary
- feature overview
- proposed architecture
- implementation components
- risks
- cost considerations
- assumptions
- open questions
